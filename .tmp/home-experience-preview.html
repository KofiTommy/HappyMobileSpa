05 / PRACTICAL DETAILS

Before you book.

How do I book?

Choose a treatment, select an available time, enter your details, and review your appointment. No account is required.

Can I change or cancel?

Use Manage booking with your reference and phone number. Online changes follow the cancellation policy shown during booking.

How do I pay?

Payment is made at the spa.

What should I tell my therapist?

Add pressure preferences, sensitive areas, pregnancy, injuries, or comfort notes to your booking, or discuss them privately when you arrive.
